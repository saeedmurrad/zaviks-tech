About

A studio that starts from a finished shelf, not a blank page.

Most software projects begin with nothing and a long estimate. We would rather begin with a product that already behaves, and spend the budget on the difference.

Why a shelf

A business owner should be able to see the thing before paying for a build. The shelf is that shop. Every item is a working starting point: a description, a way to try it or to ask for a demo, and a price that is either a starting figure or a quote.

The first three are deliberate. ParkSpace is a marketplace you can open today. Clientline is a small-business CRM. Stockroom is inventory. They cover three jobs we are asked for often: taking bookings, keeping track of customers, and knowing what is in stock. More products can be added without changing how the shelf works.

How a copy works

You license a copy for your business. Zaviks Tech keeps the base, which means a later customer still has a product to buy, and a fix to the base can be brought across to you when it helps. Your name, your data, and your configuration stay yours.

Deployment is aimed at the system you already have, whether that is a server in the office or a cloud account. We do not require you to move onto our hosting to use the product.
